Doodle

A browser extension that makes Moodle easier to use: a cleaner design, every deadline in one list, estimated grades, reminders and a built-in file viewer. Works with Moodle 4.0 and newer. Formerly Better Moodle. An independent student project, not affiliated with Moodle Pty Ltd or any university.
