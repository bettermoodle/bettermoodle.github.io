Doodle Beta — Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · for the Doodle Beta test version

Doodle is a browser extension that makes Moodle easier to use: a cleaner design and dashboard, every deadline in one list, estimated grades, reminders, a built-in file viewer, and your weekly progress and streak. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Moodle Pty Ltd or any university.

The short version

  • Everything Doodle reads stays in your browser on your own device.
  • Doodle has no server. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no selling or sharing of data.
  • It only reads the sites you allow: your Moodle, and class tools if you choose to.

What Doodle reads

While you are logged in to the Moodle you connected at setup, Doodle uses your existing Moodle session to read pages you could open yourself, to find dates, deadlines and grades:

  • Your Moodle timeline, calendar, notifications and grades
  • Your courses’ pages, assignments and quizzes (including your submission status), forum and announcement posts, Moodle pages, and the files in your courses (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OpenDocument, text and images). Scanned documents and images are read with text recognition that runs entirely on your device.
  • Syllabus pages your instructors link from Moodle, when they are public (for example Notion pages and Google Docs/Sheets/Slides)
  • Only if you allow class tools: the page of a class tool (such as Gradescope, Pearson or Top Hat) at the moment you open it yourself, to pick up the due dates shown on it
  • Only if you connect Perusall: your Perusall assignment list and due dates
  • Public syllabus data: for universities that have a public syllabus service, the grading breakdown of courses that don’t post one on Moodle. Doodle downloads the public course list and matches your course on your device; it never sends your name, courses or grades.

Doodle never reads or stores your Moodle password. When you set it up, it checks that the address you chose is a Moodle site by reading that site’s public information (its name, language and version).

What Doodle stores, and where

  • On your device (browser extension storage): the deadlines it found, your course list, which items you marked done, files you opened in its viewer, and scan history.
  • In your browser’s sync storage, if browser sync is on: your settings, course colors, nicknames, card order, your own tasks and grade weights. This is synced by your browser provider (for example Google Chrome Sync) under your own account, not by Doodle.

Uninstalling Doodle deletes all of this data.

Network requests

Doodle only connects to: your Moodle; public syllabus pages linked from your courses; class tool sites you allowed, only for the page you opened; Perusall, if you connected it; and public syllabus services (currently api.wasedatime.com; the request names your faculty only). These requests go directly from your browser to those sites. All of Doodle’s code is included in the extension; it does not download or run remote code.
