Better Moodle Beta — Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · for the Better Moodle Beta test version

Better Moodle is a browser extension that makes Moodle easier to use: a cleaner design and dashboard, every deadline in one list, estimated grades, reminders, a built-in file viewer, and your weekly progress and streak. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Moodle Pty Ltd or any university.

The short version

  • Everything Better Moodle reads stays in your browser on your own device.
  • Your courses, deadlines, grades and files are never sent anywhere.
  • Only anonymous error reports and simple feature-use counts are sent, to fix and improve Better Moodle. You can turn the counts off.
  • No ads, no selling or sharing of data.
  • It only reads the sites you allow: your Moodle, and class tools if you choose to.

What Better Moodle reads

While you are logged in to the Moodle you connected at setup, Better Moodle uses your existing Moodle session to read pages you could open yourself, to find dates, deadlines and grades:

  • Your Moodle timeline, calendar, notifications and grades
  • Your courses’ pages, assignments and quizzes (including your submission status), forum and announcement posts, Moodle pages, and the files in your courses (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OpenDocument, text and images). Scanned documents and images are read with text recognition that runs entirely on your device.
  • Syllabus pages your instructors link from Moodle, when they are public (for example Notion pages and Google Docs/Sheets/Slides)
  • Only if you allow class tools: the page of a class tool (such as Gradescope, Pearson or Top Hat) at the moment you open it yourself, to pick up the due dates shown on it
  • Only if you connect Perusall: your Perusall assignment list and due dates
  • Public syllabus data: for universities that have a public syllabus service, the grading breakdown of courses that don’t post one on Moodle. Better Moodle downloads the public course list and matches your course on your device; it never sends your name, courses or grades.

Better Moodle never reads or stores your Moodle password. When you set it up, it checks that the address you chose is a Moodle site by reading that site’s public information (its name, language and version).

What Better Moodle stores, and where

  • On your device (browser extension storage): the deadlines it found, your course list, which items you marked done, files you opened in its viewer, and scan history.
  • In your browser’s sync storage, if browser sync is on: your settings, course colors, nicknames, card order, your own tasks and grade weights. This is synced by your browser provider (for example Google Chrome Sync) under your own account, not by Better Moodle.

Uninstalling Better Moodle deletes all of this data.

Error reports and usage counts

To find and fix problems, Better Moodle sends two small kinds of anonymous data. Neither ever includes your courses, assignment titles, deadlines, grades, files, name, email address or Moodle login.

  • Error reports (always on): when something in Better Moodle breaks, a short description of the error, the extension version, your school’s Moodle address, version and country, and your language. Course names and personal details are removed before anything is sent. Sent to Sentry.
  • Usage counts (on by default, can be turned off): which features get used, such as opening the calendar or exporting deadlines, as simple counts. Sent to PostHog without your IP address. Turn them off any time in the popup under More settings → Share usage stats. At schools in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland they are off unless you turn them on.

Both use a random ID created when you install Better Moodle, which isn’t linked to you. This data is used only to fix and improve Better Moodle. It is never sold, shared for advertising or used to identify you. Reports are kept for up to 90 days (usage counts up to 1 year) and then deleted.

Network requests

Better Moodle only connects to: your Moodle; public syllabus pages linked from your courses; class tool sites you allowed, only for the page you opened; Perusall, if you connected it; public syllabus services (currently api.wasedatime.com; the request names your faculty only); and, for error reports and usage counts, Sentry (sentry.io) and PostHog (posthog.com). These requests go directly from your browser to those sites. All of Better Moodle’s code is included in the extension; it does not download or run remote code.
